# Launch campaign: reels

Four reels, four styles. Post them, watch which style holds attention, then make more of the winner.
The videos are in [`reels/`](reels/).

## Before posting

- **Link in bio** must open the app's sign-up page, and the app must be live.
- **"DM us"** in reel 4: someone needs to answer DMs from brands quickly.
- **Strava**: reels 1, 3 and 4 say results are "checked with Strava". Strava has brand rules for
  apps that use its data. Read them before running *paid* ads, and change the wording if needed.
- **Made-up examples**: Maya, Leo and the riders in reels 2 and 4 are examples, and those reels
  say "Illustrative example" on screen. Swap in real athletes (with their permission) once you have them.
- **Heart fact** (reel 1): "up to 20 litres a minute" is the peak figure usually quoted for
  well-trained athletes. Keep the "up to".

## Posting order (launch week)

| Day | Reel | Why this slot |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Launch day | 02 Beginner beats a pro | The boldest hook and the clearest idea. Pin it to the profile |
| Day 2 | 01 Cross the line | Science explainers get saved and shared |
| Day 4 | 03 Ghost race | Speaks to runners who already use Strava |
| Day 6 | 02 again, as a paid boost if it led on watch time | Spend money on what already works |
| Any time | 04 For brands | LinkedIn, and attached to every brand email |

Instagram and TikTok both work. Post as a Reel/TikTok, not a feed video. Pick each reel's
`cover.jpg` as the thumbnail.

## What to measure after 48 hours

- **3-second hold**: the share of viewers still watching after 3 s. This tells you if the hook works.
- **Average watch time / completion**: tells you if the story holds.
- **Shares and saves**: tells you if people want to send it to friends.
- **Profile visits and link clicks**: tells you if it turns into sign-ups.

Make more reels in the style that wins on hold rate and link clicks. To make a variation,
change the captions in that reel's `reel.js` (for example a swim or hike version of reel 3) and
re-export it.
