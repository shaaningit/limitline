LIMITLINE · L MARK

Eight directions for a single L. Each shown large, then at 64, 32 and 16 px, reversed on volt, and as an app icon.

01Check

The foot snaps up into a tick: a quest, done. Reads as L and as a checkmark.

02Cut

Heavy L sliced on the diagonal. Sharp, technical, sportswear energy.

03Sprint

Italic L with speed slits. Pure motion, closest to a running brand.

04Route

One continuous route line, start ring to finish dot. Calm, outdoorsy.

05Frame

A camera viewfinder corner with a record dot. Train it, post it.

06Tile

The L cut out of a solid square. Made to be an app icon first.

07Step

The foot climbs in steps. Every quest is a level up.

08Fold

A band folded at the corner, lit like paper. Premium, quiet.

Volt #CEFF00 on matte black #121212. All but Fold are a single colour, so they work on kit, stickers and embroidery.
